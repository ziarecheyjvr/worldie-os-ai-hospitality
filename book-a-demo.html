Frequently asked questions.

Is Worldie bespoke?

Your setup is. We tailor the features, AI specialists, knowledge and workflows around your business, using the Worldie OS platform.

You get a personalised implementation without developing a new system from scratch.

Does it keep learning?

Worldie identifies questions it couldn’t answer and sends management daily suggestions for improving its knowledge.

Your team reviews the gaps and adds the correct information, which Worldie can use in future conversations. It improves with your business, while you remain in control of the knowledge it uses.

Do we need every feature?

No. We configure the capabilities relevant to your operation. The aim is to give you what you need—not overwhelm your team with what you don’t.

Does it replace our team?

Worldie supports your people by handling routine conversations and helping organise the work behind them. Questions and decisions that need a person come back to your team with context.

Can it make bookings?

Worldie supports bookings, booking requests and booking links, depending on your setup. Approval requirements are agreed during configuration; bookings through connected systems require staff approval.

Does it work with our existing systems?

We’ll review your systems during the demo and confirm which connections are supported, what is involved and any limitations before agreeing your setup.

How long does onboarding take?

That depends on your channels, integrations, information and configuration. We’ll agree the scope and launch plan with you before getting started.

How much does it cost?

Pricing reflects your agreed setup and requirements. We’ll scope the right configuration with you and provide a proposal.
