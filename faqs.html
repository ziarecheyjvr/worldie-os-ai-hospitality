A few things worth knowing.

Is Worldie genuinely bespoke?

Your setup is tailored to your business: its knowledge, tone, services, AI specialists, workflows, connections and approval rules. Worldie OS provides the technology underneath. We configure the solution around you rather than developing a new system from scratch each time.

Which businesses is Worldie for?

Restaurants, bars, spas, members’ clubs, marinas, yacht businesses, hotels and other accommodation providers, tours and experiences. Worldie is built for hospitality, with a setup that reflects the operation you actually run.

Is this just an AI chatbot?

No. Worldie connects creator partnerships, enquiries, customer records, bookings, team requests and management reporting. The value is in what happens before and after the conversation—not simply in producing a reply.

How does the Creator Bridge fit in?

It connects hospitality businesses and creators through partnerships, shared deliverables, tracked referrals and commission arrangements. Referred enquiries can flow into Worldie’s customer conversations and booking journey, with recorded results visible to both sides.

How does Worldie learn about our business?

We build its starting knowledge from the information you provide and the sources you choose to connect. Guided setup, documents, website content and other agreed inputs help it understand your services and standards.

Does it keep learning?

Worldie can remember customer preferences, identify recurring questions and flag gaps in its business knowledge. Your team receives daily suggestions, reviews what is missing and supplies the correct information. That allows better-informed replies without handing control of your policies to the AI.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

Worldie is designed to flag uncertainty and bring the question back to your team. Reply checks help identify unsupported information. Where a person needs to act, the request is handed over with context and the knowledge gap can be reviewed.

Will it replace our people?

Worldie supports your team with routine enquiries, coordination and follow-up. Your people remain responsible for service decisions, approvals and the situations that need human judgement.

Can it handle phone calls and emails?

Yes. Voice and email can form part of your setup alongside website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and SMS. We agree the relevant channels and handover routes with you.

Can it make bookings and take payments?

Depending on your setup, Worldie can support direct bookings, booking requests or links to your existing booking journey, plus customer payment links. Bookings made in connected systems require staff approval before confirmation.

Will it work with our existing systems?

We review your systems and confirm the supported connections, account requirements and functions available. The scope is agreed before implementation, including any additional work needed.

Can we use it across several locations?

Yes. Each location can have its own knowledge, AI team and operating setup. Shared customer profiles and group insight can be configured where appropriate, with access and responsibilities agreed across the business.

Can we choose which capabilities we use?

Yes. We configure the capabilities relevant to your operation. You do not have to adopt every feature, and we can review the setup as your needs change.

How is customer information handled?

Worldie provides separate business environments, role-based access, encrypted connection credentials and tools for handling data access and deletion requests. We discuss deployment, permissions and data handling as part of your setup.

How long does onboarding take?

That depends on your information, channels, integrations and workflows. We agree a scope and launch plan before starting, then guide your team through configuration, testing and onboarding.

How much does it cost?

Pricing reflects your agreed setup, requirements and ongoing service. We scope the solution with you and provide a proposal before work begins.
